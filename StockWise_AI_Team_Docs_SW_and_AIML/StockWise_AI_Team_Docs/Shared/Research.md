# Research and Evidence Log

## Validate
1. Exact official PS2 requirements.
2. Dataset source, license, provenance, columns, granularity and limitations.
3. Forecasting baseline and chronological evaluation design.
4. Metric selection: MAE/WAPE/bias where appropriate; avoid MAPE when actuals can be zero.
5. Optimization objective, units, assumptions and feasibility constraints.
6. Hosting limits, cost, data privacy and regional requirements.
7. Provider terms, credentials, rate limits and costs for optional integrations.
8. Interview 5–10 potential users if feasible; record anonymized findings with permission.
