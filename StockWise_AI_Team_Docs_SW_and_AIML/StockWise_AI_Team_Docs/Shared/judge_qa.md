# Judge Q&A

**What problem?** Distribution teams balance availability, inventory cost, transfers and expiry across locations.

**What differentiates it?** A connected workflow combining forecasts, inventory rules, network-aware constrained allocation, expiry considerations, baseline comparison, approval and audit—not forecasting alone. Verify novelty against PS and competitors.

**Why AI/ML?** Forecasting estimates future demand. It is evaluated against a baseline and informs deterministic inventory calculations and constrained optimization.

**How do you know it works?** Chronological backtesting, appropriate metrics and baseline comparison. Quote only measured results; use baseline if data is insufficient.

**How does optimization work?** A defined objective under implemented supply, demand, capacity, cost, lead-time and expiry constraints; feasibility is validated.

**What if data is poor?** Show warnings, qualify/block unsafe decisions and use documented fallback.

**Does it place real orders?** Only when a configured integration confirms it. Otherwise it records an internal action; consequential actions need approval.

**How do you reduce expiry?** Estimate sell-through before expiry using batch/demand/transit data; risk is not proof of prevented waste.

**How measure impact?** Compare forecast error, stockouts, waste, transfer cost and planning time against a documented baseline. Business impact requires a pilot.

**Limitations?** Data quality, stockout-censored sales, promotions, uncertain lead times and the gap between recommendation and execution. Demo may use synthetic data.

**Next steps?** Pilot validation, POS/ERP integration and improvements based on measured evidence.
